# Expert DNA PowerPoint template: how to use it

The file `expert-dna-template.pptx` holds 18 ready layouts for proposals and training material. Each layout has one example slide filled in, so you can see how it is meant to look.

## 1. Install the fonts first (once per computer)

Without the fonts, PowerPoint swaps in a plain font and the slides lose their look.

1. Open the `fonts` folder (next to this folder).
2. Select all five .ttf files, right-click, choose "Install for all users".
3. Restart PowerPoint.

## 2. Add a new slide from a layout

1. In PowerPoint, go to the Home tab.
2. Click the small arrow under "New Slide".
3. Pick a layout by its number and name, for example "12 Offer and price".
4. Click inside each box and type. The grey hint text disappears as soon as you type.

To change the layout of an existing slide: Home tab, "Layout", pick another one.

## 3. The 18 layouts

| No. | Layout | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Title | First slide of a proposal or a course |
| 02 | Agenda | What the meeting or lesson covers |
| 03 | Section divider | Start of a new part (the only full orange slide: use it sparingly) |
| 04 | Statement | One big idea in one sentence |
| 05 | Content with label | Text or a short list |
| 06 | Two columns | Two ideas side by side |
| 07 | Three cards | Three options, parts or benefits |
| 08 | Process in 4 steps | How the work runs |
| 09 | Timeline | Weeks or milestones |
| 10 | Comparison | Before and after, without and with |
| 11 | Scope and deliverables | What is included, as a table |
| 12 | Offer and price | Up to three options with prices |
| 13 | Quote | A client quote with photo (real words only, with permission) |
| 14 | Image and text | A real screenshot or photo with explanation |
| 15 | Lesson opener | Module and lesson number, learning goal |
| 16 | Exercise | Instructions on the left, fields to fill on the right |
| 17 | Key takeaways | Three things to remember |
| 18 | Closing and contact | Last slide with next step and contact |

## 4. Rules that keep it looking right

- Replace everything in curly braces, for example {Client name}, before sending.
- Small labels above headlines are written in CAPITAL LETTERS.
- Body text stays at 16 pt. If text does not fit, cut words, do not shrink the font.
- Use the orange section slide at most once or twice per presentation.
- No stock photos and no clip art. Use real screenshots or your real photo.
- Prices in the example slides are working numbers. Check them each time.
- Never type a long dash. Use a comma, a colon or a full stop.

## 5. Always send a PDF

File, Save As (or Export), choose PDF. A PDF carries the fonts inside it, so the client sees exactly what you see. Send the .pptx only to people who will edit it and have the fonts installed.
