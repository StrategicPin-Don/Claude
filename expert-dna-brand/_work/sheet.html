FINALISTS, RANKED

1. 02 Two halves. The only mark that draws the promise itself, keeps its silhouette at 16 px, and is a frontal flat brain, so it sits next to the StrategicPin side-view node brain as a relative, not a twin.
2. 03 E fold. The strongest favicon and the most ownable shape; the brain reading is weaker and needs the story.
3. 05 Wordmark first. Calm and premium, but the idea is subtle; best used as a detail inside the wordmark of the winner.
